Pure Monocular Vision Geometry Pipeline

1. Vanishing Point Alignment (Heading Error): The intersection of runway side edge lines yields vanishing point V_p = (u_v, v_v). Relative heading crab angle is Δψ = arctan((u_v - c_x) / f_x).

2. Inverse Perspective Mapping (IPM): Computes homography H mapping the perspective trapezoid to an orthorectified top-down bird's-eye view inset shown on the top-right of each HUD frame.

3. Aspect Ratio & Dimension Sufficiency: Using the perspective depth ratio between threshold and far end, the engine computes visible length-to-width ratio L/W invariant to unknown absolute height.

4. Dual-Class Decision Engine: Simultaneously evaluates whether the strip meets the stringent certification standards of heavy jet transport versus the agile rough-field requirements of fixed-wing RC planes/UAVs.
